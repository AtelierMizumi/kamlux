Auto Brightness for KDE Plasma

Smart screen brightness that adapts to your environment using your camera

Get Started Learn More

Why Kamlux?

📷

Camera-Based

Uses your webcam's auto-exposure to detect ambient light levels - no extra hardware needed

🎯

KDE Native

Integrates directly with KDE Plasma's brightness control via qdbus

✨

Smooth Transitions

Exponential moving average prevents jarring brightness changes

🔧

Fully Configurable

Customize brightness curves, intervals, and response speed to your preference

Quick Start

curl -sL https://AtelierMizumi.github.io/kamlux/install-arch.sh | bash

That's it! The installer will guide you through the setup.

How It Works

1

Detect Light

Your webcam's auto-exposure automatically adjusts to room lighting

2

Calculate

Kamlux reads gain & exposure values to compute darkness score

3

Adjust

Screen brightness smoothly adapts to match your environment
